## 📈 How to Read the Leaderboard

### For Binary Problems (e.g., 2-SAT)
- **Ranking**: By `total_work` (ascending - lower is better)
- **Status**: "Solved" means the solution satisfies the problem constraints
- **Not Solved**: Solutions that don't satisfy constraints appear at the bottom

### For Continuous Problems
- **Ranking**: By `player_score` (descending - higher is better)
- **Score**: Based on player-defined decoding policy
